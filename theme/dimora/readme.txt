A calm, editorial block theme for interior designers, architects and small design studios.

== Description ==

Dimora is a block theme for interior design studios. Large serif titles, generous white space and photographs that are given room.

The home page is ready as soon as the theme is active: a statement hero, a sideways gallery of projects, services that open, the studio in numbers, the process in five steps, a client quote, the journal and a running call to action. It is built from patterns, so every section can also be added to any page.

What is included:

* 11 page patterns: Home, Projects, six project case studies, Studio, Services and Contact
* 29 section patterns, among them a project page in six parts (title and facts, story in three chapters, gallery, palette of materials, words of the client, link to the next project), a drawing-to-room slider and a material library
* two headers (menu in a row, or a full-screen menu) and two footers
* four style variations: Oxblood, Olive, Plaster and Ink (dark)
* templates for pages, pages without title, articles, the blog, archives, search and 404
* scroll animations made for the theme, without a page builder: titles that rise word by word, pictures that unveil, a pinned sideways gallery, counting numbers, a running line of text and a header that steps aside while you read

Everything is made of core blocks. Colours, fonts and every text can be changed in the Site Editor.

= The home page =

Until you choose a static front page, the front page shows the ready-made sections of the theme. To edit them as a normal page, create a page, choose the pattern "Home" for it and set it as your homepage in Settings > Reading: from then on the front page shows that page.

= Animations =

Animations can be switched off in Appearance > Customize > Animations. Visitors who ask their device for reduced motion never see them. If JavaScript does not run, all content is simply visible.

= Project pages =

Create a page, choose one of the "Project" patterns and give it the template "Page without title", because the pattern brings its own title. Replace the photographs and the texts, and change the four colours of the palette with the background colour of each swatch.

= Contact forms =

A theme cannot bring a contact form. The Contact pattern shows your details and two buttons; put the form block of the form plugin you prefer in their place.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How do I get the pages of the demo? =

Every page of the demo is a page pattern: create a page and choose the pattern in the window that opens. The companion plugin "Dimora Starter" does this for all pages in one click, and also creates the menu and three sample articles.

= How do I change the big name in the footer? =

It is the title of your site (Settings > General). It always fills the width of the footer, whatever its length.

= How do I change the label that follows the pointer? =

It is a translatable text ("View"). The sideways gallery, the label and smooth scrolling only run with a mouse or trackpad; on touch screens the gallery is swiped.
